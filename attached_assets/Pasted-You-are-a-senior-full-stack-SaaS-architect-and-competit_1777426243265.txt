You are a senior full-stack SaaS architect and competitive intelligence systems engineer.

Your task is to design and build an MVP for a SaaS product called:

"MediaIntel – Competitive Media & PR Opportunity Engine"

## 🧠 Product Overview

This tool helps marketing and growth teams identify:
- Where competitors are being mentioned in top-tier media
- Which publishers are covering them
- Recent PR opportunities that can be used for positioning and outreach

It focuses on Tier-1 and Tier-2 media publications only.

## 🎯 Target Competitors (hardcoded for MVP)
- WP Engine
- Kinsta
- Hostinger
- GoDaddy
- Bluehost
- Pagely
- Flywheel

## 🎯 Primary User (ICP)
- SaaS marketing teams
- Growth marketers
- PR & communications teams
- Hosting / infrastructure companies (e.g., Cloudways)

---

## 🔥 Core Functionality

### 1. Competitor Mention Tracking
System must detect mentions of competitors in:
- News articles
- Blogs
- Press releases
- Tech publications

Sources must include (but not limited to):
- Tier 1 tech media
- SaaS blogs
- Hosting review sites
- Industry publications

---

### 2. Time-Based Filtering
Every mention must be categorized into:

- Last 7 days
- Last 30 days
- Last 90 days

Each mention must include:
- Title
- Source (publisher name)
- URL
- Date
- Context snippet (1–3 lines)
- Competitor mentioned

---

### 3. Media Placement Intelligence Layer

For each mention, analyze:

- Why was the competitor mentioned?
- Was it:
  - Product review
  - Comparison article
  - Ranking list
  - Industry news
  - Customer story
  - Sponsored content

Tag each accordingly.

---

### 4. Opportunity Detection Engine (IMPORTANT)

For every competitor mention, generate:

- “PR Gap Opportunity for Cloudways”
- Suggested angle Cloudways can use
- Suggested outreach target type (blog, journalist, affiliate site)

Example output:
"If Hostinger is featured in 'Top 10 Cheap Hosting Providers', Cloudways opportunity = premium performance-focused counter positioning"

---

### 5. Dashboard Requirements (MVP UI)

Build a simple SaaS dashboard:

- Left sidebar:
  - Competitors list
  - Time filters (7/30/90 days)

- Main panel:
  - Feed of mentions
  - Each card shows:
    - Competitor
    - Title
    - Publisher
    - Date
    - Category tag
    - Opportunity insight

- Filters:
  - Publisher type
  - Mention type (review, comparison, news)

---
